Offline speech recognition components

OpenAI Whisper base.en model, converted to GGML by the whisper.cpp project.
Model identity and pinned source revision are in whisper-model.json.
https://github.com/openai/whisper
MIT license: WHISPER-MODEL-LICENSE.txt

Whisper.net 1.9.1, by Sandro Hanea and contributors.
Managed .NET bindings and the Whisper.net.Runtime CPU native package.
https://github.com/sandrohanea/whisper.net
MIT license: WHISPER-NET-LICENSE.txt

whisper.cpp, by Georgi Gerganov and contributors.
Native inference implementation used by Whisper.net.Runtime.
https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp
MIT license: WHISPER-CPP-LICENSE.txt

The model is prepared during development and bundled with the harness.
The application does not download models or send recordings to a cloud service.
